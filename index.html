Mentoring

A mentor from day one.

Every newcomer gets an older student who has been through the same courses, the same exams and the same doubts.

Get matched

New students are paired with a mentor from an older year at the start of the year.

2

Ask anything

Courses, exams, internships, choosing a program after prépa: your mentor shares what worked for them.

3

Pass it on

Once you are ahead, you mentor the next students and add your notes to the shared resources.
